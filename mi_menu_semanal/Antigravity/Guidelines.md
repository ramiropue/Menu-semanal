# Design System Strategy: Professional Contrast

## Vision & Narrative
This design system is built on a foundation of professional stability balanced with high-energy accents. By pairing deep teal and slate-grey tones with vibrant, warm oranges, we create a visual language that feels established yet dynamic. The system is designed for clarity and functional elegance, suitable for complex dashboards or professional tools.

## Visual Language

### Color Palette
- **Primary Foundation**: The system utilizes a deep Teal (#00695C) for primary actions and brand presence, conveying reliability and focus.
- **Secondary Energy**: A vivid Deep Orange (#E65100) serves as the secondary color, providing high-contrast cues for interactive elements that require immediate attention.
- **Tertiary Accent**: Soft Coral (#FFAB91) acts as a decorative or highlighting agent, softening the high-contrast relationship between teal and orange.
- **Neutral Surface**: Blue Grey (#455A64) provides a sophisticated backdrop for surfaces and typography, moving away from stark blacks or greys for a more refined feel.

### Typography
- **Headlines**: Plus Jakarta Sans is used for headings, offering a modern, clean, and slightly geometric feel that maintains professional authority.
- **Body & Labels**: Manrope is the workhorse for body copy and UI labels. Its contemporary sans-serif structure ensures maximum readability across varying pixel densities.

### Shape & Structure
- **Roundedness**: A moderate radius (Level 2) is applied to all UI components. This creates a friendly but disciplined interface that avoids the starkness of sharp corners without feeling overly informal.
- **Layout & Spacing**: A normal spacing scale (Level 2) is employed to ensure a balanced information density. It provides enough breathing room for clarity while maintaining a high functional utility for data-rich environments.

## Component Execution
Buttons and key call-to-actions should leverage the primary teal for standard operations, while the secondary orange is reserved for primary conversion points or critical feedback loops. Surfaces should remain light and airy to let the sophisticated palette drive the user experience.

# Priorities

- Work with component and style reusability in mind.
- Create components for cards or any other repeated elements.
- Organize folders and subfolders according to the pages you are working on.
- Do not configure or install any libraries without asking first.
